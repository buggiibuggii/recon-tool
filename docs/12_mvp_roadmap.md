# 12. MVP Roadmap

## Goal

Launch a privacy-first legal-awareness MVP with authentication, legal chat, constitution explorer, legal guides, evidence metadata, OCR summaries, and localization foundation.

## Milestones

1. Weeks 1-2: Backend auth, database migration, guide/article ingestion pipeline.
2. Weeks 3-4: AI chat with citations, ChromaDB retrieval, safety guardrails.
3. Weeks 5-6: Expo app navigation, language switcher, chat, guide, constitution screens.
4. Weeks 7-8: Evidence vault metadata, OCR upload, readiness checklist, security review.
